## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a Master's student in Computer Science & Engineering at **Santa Clara University**, and I work as a Systems Developer Intern at the **Miller Center for Global Impact**, building retrieval and memory systems that answer only from cited documents. Before grad school I spent almost two years at **Jio Platforms** building Angular micro-frontends and dashboards for 100+ clients.

I like problems where an AI system has to be *right*, not just fluent: retrieval you can trust, agents that know when to hand off to a human, and pipelines that survive real document sets. I also love hackathons, since nothing focuses you like a deadline.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

| | Project | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 🥈 | **Relaynt** | A swarm of specialized LLM agents that watches CI/CD pipelines, scores failures by severity, retries what it can, and escalates critical issues to a human-approval dashboard. *2nd place, Apify × Scalekit Hackathon.* |
| 🏆 | **Vanity** | An agentic AI driving coach with a personalized training simulation and real-time feedback. *Winner (5th place), Beta Fund AI Super Hackathon.* |
| 🧠 | **Recognize** | Meeting intelligence in the browser. A Chrome extension captures live audio, and the people, decisions, and action items land in a searchable knowledge graph (GraphRAG on Neo4j). |
| 🎬 | **[FrameChat](https://devpost.com/software/framechat)** | A video understanding platform built on multi-modal RAG, with smart frame sampling that keeps analysis fast and cheap. |
| 🩺 | **[HealinMotion](https://github.com/Rishi181020/AI-Driven-Parkinsons-Actvity-Recommendation)** | Wearable-sensor freezing-of-gait detection for Parkinson's patients, plus a MedGemma chatbot that explains each activity recommendation. |
